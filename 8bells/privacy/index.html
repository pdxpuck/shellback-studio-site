8 Bells — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

What we collect

Nothing — no personal data of any kind.

Network

8 Bells has no network permission (no INTERNET). It cannot transmit anything, to anyone, ever.

Stored on your device

Your settings only, kept in Android’s standard SharedPreferences. They never leave your device, and uninstalling the app removes them:

  • bells on/off (master switch)
  • bells volume
  • quiet hours (enabled, start time, end time)
  • strike cadence (two spacing values)
  • watch mode (US / Royal Navy dog watches)
  • silent-mode preference

Permissions

8 Bells asks for exactly two:

  • USE_EXACT_ALARM — schedules the half-hour strikes so they ring exactly on time. That is its only purpose.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-arms the schedule after your phone restarts, so strikes continue without reopening the app.
